At the start, play the music "A3/2 A3/2 C4/2 A3/2 E4/2 A3/2 D4/2 C4/2 A3/2 A3/2 C4/2 A3/2 G4/2 E4/2 D4 -" at 150 beats per minute as a saw wave and show "COINS {score} / 20" at the top left in "#ffffff".
Move the player sideways with the arrow keys at 7.5 units per second and face where it moves.
The player jumps with space at 14.5 units per second, higher while held, with the jump sound.
Animate the player with "hero_1" and "hero_2" at 8 frames per second while it walks.
Each slime moves toward the player sideways at 2.51 units per second within 9 units.
Animate each slime with "slime_1" and "slime_2" at 4 frames per second.
Each bat moves toward the player at 3.65 units per second within 9 units.
Animate each bat with "bat_1" and "bat_2" at 6 frames per second.
Animate each coin with "coin_1" and "coin_2" at 4 frames per second.
Animate each flag with "flag_1" and "flag_2" at 4 frames per second.
When the player touches a coin, destroy it, add 1 to the score, squash the player and play the coin sound.
When the player lands on a slime, destroy it, bounce the player up at 11 units per second, squash the player and play the bounce sound.
When the player lands on a bat, destroy it, bounce the player up at 11 units per second, squash the player and play the bounce sound.
When the player touches a slime, the player loses.
When the player touches a bat, the player loses.
When the player touches a spike, the player loses.
When the player falls below -12, the player loses.
When the player touches the flag, the player wins "LEVEL CLEAR".
When the player presses r, restart the game.

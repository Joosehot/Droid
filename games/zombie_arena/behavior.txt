At the start, the player has 3 lives, play the music "C4/2 E4/2 G4/2 E4/2 F4/2 A4/2 C5/2 A4/2 G4/2 B4/2 D5/2 B4/2 C5 - G4/2 E4/2 C4" at 132 beats per minute as a square wave, show "SCORE {score}" at the top left in "#f2f2f2", show "LIVES {lives}" at the top right in "#f2f2f2", show "TIME {countdown 60}" at the top in "#f2f2f2" and show "WAVE {wave}" at the bottom in "#f2f2f2".
Move the player with the arrow keys at 7 units per second and face where it moves.
Animate the player with "hero_1" and "hero_2" at 8 frames per second while it walks.
Each zombie moves toward the player at 2.3 units per second.
Animate each zombie with "zombie_1", "zombie_2" and "zombie_3" at 6 frames per second.
When the player touches a gem, destroy it, add 5 points, squash the player and play the pickup sound.
When the player touches a zombie, the player loses a life, safe for 1.5 seconds.
Every 15 seconds, start the next wave of zombies.
When the time reaches 60 seconds, the player wins "YOU SURVIVED".
When the player presses r, restart the game.

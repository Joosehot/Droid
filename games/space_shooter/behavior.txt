At the start, the player has 3 lives, play the music "C4/2 E4/2 G4/2 E4/2 F4/2 A4/2 C5/2 A4/2 G4/2 B4/2 D5/2 B4/2 C5 - G4/2 E4/2 C4" at 132 beats per minute as a square wave, show "SCORE {score}" at the top left in "#ffffff", show "LIVES {lives}" at the top right in "#ffffff" and show "WAVE {wave}" at the top in "#ffffff".
Move the player sideways with the arrow keys at 11 units per second within 10.78 units.
The player shoots up with space at 22 units per second every 0.28 seconds, using "shot", with the laser sound.
Each alien marches sideways at 1.4 units per second within 12.5 units, stepping down 0.6 units, up to 2.5 times as fewer are left, 15 percent faster each wave.
Every 1.6 seconds, a random alien shoots down at 9 units per second, using "bomb".
When a shot hits an alien, destroy both, add 30 points and play the explosion sound.
When a shot hits a shield, destroy the shot.
When a bomb hits a shield, destroy both.
When a bomb hits the player, destroy the bomb and the player loses a life, safe for 1.5 seconds.
When no aliens are left, destroy the bombs and start the next wave of aliens.
When the score reaches 2160, the player wins "ALL WAVES CLEARED".
When an alien falls below -6, the player loses "INVADED".
When the player presses r, restart the game.

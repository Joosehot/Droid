== behavior.txt
 1. [music/bounce + hud/corner + health/lives] At the start, the player has 3 lives, play the music "C4/2 E4/2 G4/2 E4/2 F4/2 A4/2 C5/2 A4/2 G4/2 B4/2 D5/2 B4/2 C5 - G4/2 E4/2 C4" at 132 beats per minute as a square wave, show "COINS {score} / 14" at the top left in "#ffffff" and show "LIVES {lives}" at the top right in "#ffffff".
 2. [controls/snappy] Move the player sideways with the arrow keys at 7.5 units per second and face where it moves.
 3. [controls/snappy] The player jumps with space at 14.5 units per second, higher while held, with the jump sound.
 4. [juice/full] Animate the player with "hero_1" and "hero_2" at 8 frames per second while it walks.
 5. [enemy_behavior/by_kind (slime: patrol)] Each slime patrols 2.5 units to each side at 2.2 units per second.
 6. [juice/full] Animate each slime with "slime_1" and "slime_2" at 4 frames per second.
 7. [juice/full] Animate each coin with "coin_1" and "coin_2" at 4 frames per second.
 8. [pickups/rewarding] When the player touches a coin, destroy it, add 1 to the score, squash the player and play the coin sound.
 9. [genre/platformer (stomping)] When the player lands on a slime, destroy it, bounce the player up at 11 units per second, squash the player and play the bounce sound.
10. [health/lives] When the player touches a slime, the player loses a life, safe for 1.5 seconds.
11. [layout.platformer.kill_y] When the player falls below -12, the player loses a life and goes back to the start.
12. [goal/collect_all] When no coins are left, the player wins "ALL COLLECTED".
13. [every game restarts] When the player presses r, restart the game.

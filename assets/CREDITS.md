## Fallbacks

Every kind the library must cover has downloaded art; no text-sprite or procedural (SDF)
fallback is used. Gnome, cat and frog are covered in 2D; in 3D there is a cat (Cube Pets) but no
dedicated gnome or frog model (not required); the humanoid models can stand in for a gnome.
